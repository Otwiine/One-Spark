One Spark is a nonprofit initiative based in Uganda dedicated to empowering underserved youth through education, mentorship, and vocational training.

Uganda has one of the youngest populations globally. Yet youth face high unemployment, limited access to quality education, and widespread poverty. One Spark responds with a scalable, community-driven model that builds human capital and fosters inclusive development.

Mission

To ignite the potential of underserved youth in Uganda through education, mentorship, and vocational training — empowering them to become capable leaders who contribute to sustainable community development.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth driving transformative change across Uganda and beyond.

Why One Spark?

Connecting youth to brighter futures

In Uganda, thousands of young people leave school without the opportunities, skills, or support needed to secure meaningful livelihoods. Poverty, limited access to education, and unemployment continue to hold back immense potential.

One Spark exists to change that reality by connecting youth to education, mentorship, vocational training, and entrepreneurship opportunities that empower them to build brighter futures.

When a young person succeeds, families thrive, communities strengthen, and nations grow.

What We Do

Our Programs

One Spark delivers a comprehensive set of interventions designed to equip youth with education, practical skills, and pathways to sustainable livelihoods.

01

Scholarship & Learning Support

Provision of school supplies and scholarships to reduce financial barriers and expand access to education. We cover tuition, registration, certification fees, textbooks, stationery, uniforms, and vocational toolkits.

02

Tutoring & Mentorship

Structured after-school programs that strengthen academic performance and connect youth with mentors for guidance, career advice, and personal development. Role models matter.

03

Vocational Training

Hands-on workshops in market-relevant fields — tailoring, carpentry, ICT, agriculture, and mechanics — ensuring alignment with local labour market demands.

04

Life Skills & Entrepreneurship

Training sessions that build leadership, financial literacy, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial capacity — preparing youth for both employment and self-employment.

05

Community Learning Centres

Accessible hubs where youth can receive training, mentorship, and resources in safe, supportive environments. Rooted in the communities they serve.

06

Monitoring & Evaluation

Robust systems to track progress, measure outcomes, and ensure accountability — enabling continuous improvement and demonstrating long-term impact to partners and donors.

Who We Serve

Target Beneficiaries

We primarily serve out-of-school youth aged 13–25, with a strong emphasis on girls and young people with disabilities who face heightened barriers to education and employment.

The program extends to vulnerable populations including orphans and children from economically disadvantaged families. By addressing both immediate needs and long-term development, One Spark ensures that marginalised youth are equipped with practical skills, educational opportunities, and pathways to self-reliance.
