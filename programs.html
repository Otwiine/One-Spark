What We Do

Our Programs

One Spark delivers a comprehensive set of interventions designed to equip youth with education, practical skills, and pathways to sustainable livelihoods.

01

Scholarship & Learning Support

Provision of school supplies and scholarships to reduce financial barriers and expand access to education. We cover tuition, registration, certification fees, textbooks, stationery, uniforms, and vocational toolkits.

02

Tutoring & Mentorship

Structured after-school programs that strengthen academic performance and connect youth with mentors for guidance, career advice, and personal development. Role models matter.

03

Vocational Training

Hands-on workshops in market-relevant fields — tailoring, carpentry, ICT, agriculture, and mechanics — ensuring alignment with local labour market demands.

04

Life Skills & Entrepreneurship

Training sessions that build leadership, financial literacy, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial capacity — preparing youth for both employment and self-employment.

05

Community Learning Centres

Accessible hubs where youth can receive training, mentorship, and resources in safe, supportive environments. Rooted in the communities they serve.

06

Monitoring & Evaluation

Robust systems to track progress, measure outcomes, and ensure accountability — enabling continuous improvement and demonstrating long-term impact to partners and donors.

Implementation

Pilot: Namugongo, Kampala

The initiative launches with a pilot program in Namugongo, Kampala (Central Region), serving as a model for replication and scale across Uganda.

Local Schools

Student identification, academic support, and integration of mentorship programs.

Vocational Institutes

Hands-on training delivery aligned with labour market needs and industry standards.

Community Leaders & Business Groups

Foster local engagement and promote entrepreneurship opportunities for graduates.

Government Agencies & NGOs

Alignment with national policies and leveraging complementary resources for scale.

Long-Term Thinking

Sustainability Plan

Year 1 80% donor funded
Year 3 50% donor funded + social enterprises
Year 5 Locally sustained through partnerships and income-generating projects

Income-Generating Projects

Student-run enterprises designed to provide practical experience while generating revenue to support program activities.

Community Ownership

Active involvement of parents, local leaders, and youth in program delivery to strengthen accountability and long-term sustainability.

Strategic Partnerships

Collaboration with donors, corporate sponsors, and NGOs to secure financial and technical support over the long term.

Capacity Building

Training and development of local instructors and staff to ensure quality and reduce reliance on external expertise.
